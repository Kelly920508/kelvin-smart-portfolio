SAMPLE SCRIPTS · WRITTEN FOR REAL PROJECTS

Video Ad Scripting Samples

Two 15–20 second Reels/Meta ad scripts written for real projects on this site — structured around a hook, a clear offer, and a single call to action.

ROLARTS CYBER · 15S

HOOK (0–3s): Quick zoom on a fresh stack of printed business cards. On-screen text: "Need cards printed today?"

BODY (3–10s): Cut to the counter, papers being scanned and laminated. VO: "Rolarts Cyber — right on Moi Avenue. Cards, posters, KRA services, done while you wait."

CTA (10–15s): Hand picking up finished cards. On-screen text: "Walk in or WhatsApp us →"

SMART DIGITAL GROWTH · 20S

HOOK (0–3s): A blurry phone photo of a product. On-screen text: "This is all we need from you."

BODY (3–15s): Transition to a polished finished post. VO: "Send your photos on WhatsApp — we turn them into posts, reels, and flyers. Sheng, Swahili, English — however your customers talk."

CTA (15–20s): On-screen text: "Trial: 3 posts, KES 1,000. Chat on WhatsApp →"

// ABOUT

Interdisciplinary by design, not by accident.

I started at the intersection of two questions: how does a site actually get built and shipped, and how does anyone find it once it's live? That pushed me to learn both sides properly — hand-coding responsive layouts and deploying them through Git and Netlify on one hand, and running paid audience research and Meta Ads campaigns on the other. Most of my work now lives in that overlap: a site isn't finished when it looks right, it's finished when the right people are actually arriving on it and taking action.

The same instinct shows up in how I write and teach. Good ad copy, a clear workshop explanation, and a well-structured landing page are the same skill wearing different clothes — take something that could be complicated and make it obvious. Working across English, Kiswahili, and (still-growing) Mandarin has sharpened that further: nothing forces you to say a thing simply like having to say it in more than one language. Whether I'm building, advertising, writing, or teaching, the goal is the same — a clear signal, sent to the right audience.
